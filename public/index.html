What Sid can take care of

From a weekly mow to a full backyard makeover. One local team for the lawn, the garden and the jobs you keep putting off, across Airlie Beach, Cannonvale, Proserpine and the Whitsundays.

Lawns & gardens

  • Lawn mowing

    Regular or one-off mows with edges trimmed, paths blown down and clippings taken care of.

  • Gardening & maintenance

    Weeding, pruning, hedging and mulching to keep garden beds tidy and healthy all year.

  • Weed spraying

    Targeted herbicide treatment for lawns, paths, driveways and fence lines so the weeds stay gone.

  • Hedges & trimming

    Hedges, shrubs and overgrown plants shaped back into line.

Landscaping & irrigation

  • Landscape design

    Garden layout ideas worked out with you, so you can see the plan before any work starts.

  • Construction landscaping

    The labour and materials to build it: new garden beds, edging, turf, rock and outdoor improvements.

  • Irrigation systems

    New sprinkler and drip systems installed, plus repairs and troubleshooting on the one you've got.

  • Materials & supplies

    Soil, mulch, plants and fittings sourced and delivered as part of the job, so you don't have to.

Clean-ups & property care

  • Yard clean-ups

    Overgrown blocks, rentals and pre-sale tidy-ups brought back under control.

Common questions

Which areas do you cover?

Airlie Beach, Cannonvale, Jubilee Pocket, Proserpine and the surrounding Whitsundays. If you're a bit further out, get in touch and ask.

Are quotes free?

Yes. Send a few details and photos through the form, or call Sid, and you'll get a price with no obligation.

Can I book a regular mow?

Yes. Sid can set you up on a regular schedule, or come out for one-off jobs whenever you need.

Do you take the green waste away?

Yes. Clippings, cuttings and garden waste are cleaned up and removed as part of the job.

Do you supply materials and plants?

Yes. For landscaping and irrigation jobs Sid can source the soil, mulch, plants and fittings so it's all handled in one quote.

Do you work with NDIS participants?

Yes. Sid provides garden and yard maintenance for NDIS participants. Get in touch to talk through what you need.
